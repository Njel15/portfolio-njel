About Me

My background started in creating content for brands and products, handling shooting, editing, and shaping visuals that are engaging and suitable for digital platforms. From food that needs to look tempting on screen to brand campaigns that need clear messaging, I focus on storytelling that feels natural and relatable.

Over time, I expanded into live production, directing multi-camera setups, coordinating with sound, lighting, and internal team, and ensuring events run smoothly from rundown to final output. I’m used to working in fast-paced environments where timing, teamwork, and clear communication really matter.
