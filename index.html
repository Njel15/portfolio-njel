About Me

Hi, I'm Angel.

A Video Director, Videographer, and Video Editor with more than 5 years of experience working with creative agencies, brands, events, weddings, and live productions.

For me, video is more than just beautiful visuals. Every project has a story, emotion, and purpose behind it. My role is to turn those moments into meaningful visual experiences that people can connect with.

Throughout my journey, I've been involved in various productions—from commercial content and corporate projects to weddings, church productions, and large-scale live events. I enjoy working closely with teams, developing creative concepts, directing shoots, and crafting stories through editing.

I believe that great content isn't only about how it looks, but how it makes people feel.

When I'm not behind the camera or editing timeline, I'm continuously learning new creative techniques, exploring storytelling, and helping teams create better visual content.

Let's create something meaningful together.
